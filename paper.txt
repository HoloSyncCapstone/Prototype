As a team, we used Linear to organize our tasks and keep track of what everyone was working on. Every Monday we had a Scrum meeting where we checked in on what we finished the past week. We went through each person and gave updates on what we were able to accomplish and what was still pending. Then, we made a list of tasks to complete next and assigned it to people. We also made sure to discuss if there were any blockers so we could discuss with our mentors. Our Linear was set up similar to Kanban, with tasks moving from “To Do” to “In Progress” to “Done.” We used a lot of the scrum principles taught to us in class like communicating often, breaking up the project into smaller pieces, and making sure not to get stuck on one problem for too long. This helped us stay organized and make substantial progress each week.
